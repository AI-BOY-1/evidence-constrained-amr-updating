# Public source references (data not bundled)

- BV-BRC E. coli metadata/AST and assemblies: https://www.bv-brc.org/
- ResFinder database snapshot: eecf0aa20759, 2.6.0;
  https://bitbucket.org/genomicepidemiology/resfinder_db/
- BLASTn study version: 2.17.0+; https://blast.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/
- CombiANT: Hallstrom et al. (2025), DOI 10.1371/journal.pdig.0000669;
  archive source and exact asset mappings are stated in the acquisition scripts.
  Public photos are attributed CC BY 4.0 in the manuscript; obtain them from
  their original archive and preserve the licence/attribution yourself.
- MSDeepAMR: DOI 10.3389/fmicb.2024.1361795; DRIAMS-B subset and source weights
  are independently obtained from the authors' pinned assets.

Data access and redistribution terms vary by asset. No blanket dataset licence
is granted by this source-code package. The portable synthetic example contains
no real isolates or patients. Exact MIC/CLSI version harmonization and
holder-blind new confirmation remain external evidence gaps.

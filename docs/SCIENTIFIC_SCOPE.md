# Scientific scope and limits

This repository is a methods release for retrospective computational research.
It is not a clinical decision support system, a full AMR annotation pipeline or
a prospective/holder-blind validation release.

- k31 scaled5000 sequence sketches are representations, not AMR annotations.
- Homology candidates, when used by the historical pipeline, do not prove a
  complete resistance mechanism, mutation, expression or function.
- Approximate ANI grouping is not an SNP phylogeny and does not establish
  patient or laboratory independence.
- Evidence weights are over predictive rules, not biological causes.
- The query objective minimizes a fixed predictive-disagreement surrogate. It
  is not a clinical-risk, Brier-optimality or safety objective.
- A single permutation per seed is not a statistical null distribution.
- R=0 false-susceptible rates are undefined and must stay unavailable.
- Results from the retrospective package are not regenerated by the synthetic
  demo and are not included in this repository.

Before a new study, harmonize AST/MIC standards and exact breakpoint versions,
define an independently held-out or holder-blind cohort, document data licences,
and pre-specify code/data hashes. Do not claim stable SOTA, clinical safety,
causal mechanism or journal acceptance from this code alone.
